FAQ

Quick answers

What is Not Another Event?

Not Another Event is a team that builds event planning software and custom event systems. Our platform handles guest lists, payments, seating plans and DJ requests in one place, so organisers spend less time on chaos and more time on the event.

What kinds of events do you build systems for?

Weddings, corporate events, parties, conferences and community events. If it has guests, a schedule and a budget, we can build a system around it.

How much does a custom event system cost?

It depends on scope. Use the budget slider on our contact form to tell us what you have in mind and we will prepare a proposal that fits it. There is no obligation.

How long does a project take?

Small systems can launch in a few weeks. Larger custom builds take longer. We confirm a timeline in the proposal after we hear about your event.

How do I get in touch?

Fill in the form on this page, or use the live chat. A team member replies by email, usually within one business day.
